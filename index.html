Free video lectures — how to use

Why use it

It shows one lecture at a time with no recommended videos, comments or feed. Fewer distractions means better focus. Plan what you will watch before you start, then paste only that link.

Watch a lecture
  1. Open YouTube (app or website) and find the lecture you want.
  2. Tap Share, then Copy link.
  3. Come back to this app → Lectures, press Paste (or press and hold in the box and choose Paste).
  4. Press ▶ Play lecture. The video plays right here.
Controls in the player
  • Tap the video to pause or play.
  • Tap the ⚙ gear in the player to change the video quality (lower for slow internet, higher for clear writing) and the speed.
  • Tap the full-screen button for a bigger picture. Turn your phone sideways for the best view.
  • A link with a time, like …?t=1m30s, starts the video at that point.
  • A playlist link plays the whole series one after another.
Your recent lectures

Every lecture you play is saved on this phone, with its picture and title, under “Your recent lectures”. Tap one to watch it again. Press × to remove it. They are kept on this device only.

If a video does not play

The owner of some videos does not allow them to play outside YouTube. Press Open on YouTube ↗ below the player to watch it there. You also need an internet connection.

About ads

The player does not show suggested videos from other channels. YouTube may still show its own ads on some videos; that is the video owner’s choice and this app cannot remove them.

Watching without internet (download)

This app cannot download YouTube videos, because YouTube only allows downloads through YouTube itself. To save a lecture to watch offline: press Open on YouTube ↗, then tap the Download button under the video in the YouTube app, where YouTube offers it. Choose the video quality when it asks. The saved video plays inside the YouTube app, in your Library → Downloads.

Study tips
  • Keep a notebook open and pause to write notes.
  • Watch one lecture fully before opening another.
  • Use the recent list to revise instead of searching again.

Free video lectures

Study with better focus

This page plays only the lecture you choose. There is no recommended-videos feed, no comments and no endless scrolling, so there is less to distract you and it is easier to stay on one topic. Just paste the YouTube link of the lecture you want to watch, and it plays here.

📲 For fewer ads, use the downloaded app

Install the Class X Drive app on your phone: it opens full-screen with no browser around it, so there is less to distract you and fewer ads than opening links in the browser. (Ads inside a YouTube video are decided by the video’s owner.)

On YouTube, tap Share → Copy link. Paste the link below and press Play.

Open on YouTube ↗

Want it offline? Open it on YouTube and use YouTube’s own Download button there — you can choose the video quality in the YouTube app. See the user manual for details.

Videos play through YouTube's privacy-friendly player without suggested videos from other channels. Some videos may still show YouTube's own ads — that is decided by the video's owner. If a video says it can't be played here, use “Open on YouTube”.
